The 8-Minute Decision Unlocker

Break out of analysis paralysis in under 10 minutes.

The Core Problem

Overthinking happens when you treat small, reversible choices like permanent life decisions. Waiting for 100% certainty stalls momentum and burns mental energy.

The Two-Door Rule

  • Two-Way Door (Reversible — 90% of daily choices): If it doesn't work, you can undo, adjust, or walk back through within 48 hours. Decide in 3 minutes and test.
  • One-Way Door (Irreversible — 10% of choices): High financial, legal, or permanent impact. Gather facts and consult before acting.
Key Insight: Never spend One-Way Door energy on a Two-Way Door decision.
