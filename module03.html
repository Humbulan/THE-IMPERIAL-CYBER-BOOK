The 9-Minute Attention & Recovery Reset

Clear stress residue and regain control before opening notifications.

The Core Problem

After a tense call, conflict, or heavy 90-minute work block, your nervous system stays in high-alert mode. Jumping straight into messages or social feeds fragments your attention.

The 3-Step Reset Protocol

  1. Screen-Off Downshift (3 Minutes): Step away from screens. Inhale 4s, hold 2s, exhale slowly 6s (repeat 8 times).
  2. Brain Dump (2 Minutes): Write down open loops or nagging thoughts so your mind doesn't hold onto them.
  3. Single-Action Lock (1 Minute): Pick one concrete 30-minute task before checking any notifications.

Calibration & WhatsApp Proof Builder

Badge: Deep-Focus Pass
Requirement: Complete the reset after a stressor and finish your #1 task before opening notifications (3 times across 7 days).
