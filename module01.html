MODULE 01 | TIME: 7 MINUTES | FOCUS: ENERGY & BOUNDARIES

The "No-Guilt" Boundary Reset

Stop over-explaining. Protect your energy in two sentences.

The Core Problem

Most people say "yes" when they are already overloaded because saying "no" feels rude. When they do say "no," they pad it with long apologies and excuses — which invites pushback and drains even more energy.

The 2-Sentence Boundary Formula

  • Sentence 1 — Acknowledge: Show you heard the request without apologizing.
  • Sentence 2 — State Your Limit: State what you can or cannot do, and when.
❌ Do NOT use: "I'm so sorry, I've just been completely swamped today and my schedule is crazy, maybe if I finish early I can try..."
✓ Use instead: "Thanks for flagging this. I'm at capacity for today, so I can review it tomorrow at 10:00 AM."
Badge: Boundary Shield
Requirement: Deliver a real-world boundary with zero apologies (no "sorry," "my bad," or over-explaining).
